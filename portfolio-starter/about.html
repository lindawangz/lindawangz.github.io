I’m Linda, a UCLA student studying Mathematics of Computation with a background in both art and programming. I enjoy bringing those two sides together — using visual thinking to shape an idea and code to turn it into something people can actually use.

My work sits somewhere between design and development. I care about how a product looks and feels, but also about how it works behind the scenes. Whether I’m designing an interface, building a prototype, or working through a technical problem, I like finding the balance between a thoughtful visual direction and a practical implementation.
